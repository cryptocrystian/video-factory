# Episode 1 — Production Proof #2 Plan

Episode: **How Far Will We Go to Power AI?**
Scene: **Cold Open — Why Put AI in Orbit?**
Target proof duration: **~28 seconds**
Status: **Preproduction / no paid generation authorized**

## Goal

Prove the upgraded Synthetic Frontier production system can create a scene that feels like premium filmed science documentary work rather than high-end animated stills.

Proof #2 is not “Proof #1 with a newer model.”

It must demonstrate:
- true video-native cinematography
- internal physical motion
- stable recurring production design
- evidence transformed into visual storytelling
- premium pacing
- deliberate sound architecture
- model selection justified by capability
- hard QC

## Golden question

> Would we place this beside premium network/streaming science documentary work without explaining that it was made with AI?

If no, the gate fails.

## Continuity asset

**Orbital Compute Platform A** is now the canonical recurring design.

Identity:
- modular low-Earth-orbit compute platform
- dark restrained compute blocks
- broad solar wings
- separate radiator panels
- small optical terminals
- no fantasy-spacecraft cues
- geometry must persist between shots

## Paper edit

### 00:00–00:04.5 — Hero reveal
Function: frontier wonder

Shot:
- extreme wide
- platform enters frame rather than sitting centered
- camera has physical lateral drift and inertia
- Earth and foreground move at different rates
- sunlight crests Earth limb during shot
- platform becomes readable at the endpoint

Target capability:
- geometry stability
- multi-plane parallax
- natural camera
- lighting evolution

This is the primary benchmark hero shot.

### 00:04.5–00:08.0 — Engineering investigation
Function: move from spectacle into physical machinery

Shot:
- parallel tracking pass along same platform
- radiator foreground
- compute modules midground
- Earth background
- subtle optical-terminal movement
- physically consistent reflections

Critical requirement:
It must look like a second camera angle on the same object, not a new generation of a similar object.

### 00:08.0–00:13.0 — FCC evidence
Function: prove proposal status

Treatment:
- authentic FCC source
- isolate only relevant language
- extract scale figure
- transform evidence into next visual idea

Do not:
- fake filing text
- create faux-scanning HUD
- simply pan across a PDF

### 00:13.0–00:18.0 — Project Suncatcher evidence
Function: establish that orbital compute exploration is broader than one company

Treatment:
- authentic Google source material
- restrained authored orbital diagram
- early-2027 prototype marker
- evidence-first, not logo-first

### 00:18.0–00:22.5 — Return to orbit
Function: viewer now sees opening image differently

Use:
- alternate portion/angle from the canonical orbital generation family
- no duplicated slow push
- shift toward power motivation

### 00:22.5–00:28.0 — Orbit-to-Earth bridge
Function: hand off into the terrestrial power story

Concept:
- orbit gives way to grounded power infrastructure
- ideally semantic rather than a generic zoom
- exact implementation remains subject to storyboard/animatic review

## Sound direction

Opening:
- near silence
- very low orbital/structural texture
- narration should not immediately fill frame

Engineering pass:
- subtle mechanical texture
- no exaggerated sci-fi sound

Evidence:
- score pulse begins
- source transitions get restrained sonic punctuation

End:
- low-frequency energy grows as scene approaches terrestrial power infrastructure

Do not use one continuous generic underscore.
